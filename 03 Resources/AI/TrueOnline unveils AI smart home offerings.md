TrueOnline launched TrueOnline Home Next, an AI-driven smart home platform combining fibre broadband, IoT devices, and a Thai-language AI assistant named Ami via the TrueX Home Hub. The platform integrates with over 3 million Tuya-compatible devices and includes AI CCTV with person/pet/baby recognition, smart plugs with energy monitoring, and biorhythm-adjusted smart lighting. Thailand currently has 12.3 million smart home devices in use, signaling a maturing market hungry for unified, AI-powered control systems. The Solar Smart Home Solution (Altervim) adds energy efficiency and sustainability to the stack. This represents a significant local infrastructure play bridging AI, IoT, and home automation in the Thai market.
