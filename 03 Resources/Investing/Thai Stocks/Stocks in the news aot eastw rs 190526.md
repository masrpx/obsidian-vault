Thai stock snapshot for 19 May 2026 covering AOT, EASTW, and RS. AOT (Airports of Thailand, ~$23B market cap, P/E 41.3) faces minor headwinds as Air India cuts Bangkok routes by ~25-46% July onward, though the issue is India-side fuel costs, not Thailand demand. EASTW (~$0.18B, +81.4% YTD) is the standout play — industrial water demand from data center liquid cooling is driving earnings momentum, with one DC client onboarding in 2Q26 and two more in 2H26, plus a new JV with STECON as long-term catalyst despite a stretched P/E of 89.5. RS Public Company (~$10.8M) has effectively collapsed, defaulting on interest payments triggering cross-default on THB 2.87B in borrowings, with CB caution sign and cash-upfront trading rules now imposed.
