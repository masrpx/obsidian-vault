Global risks are converging simultaneously: US-China summit yielded tactical de-escalation but no resolution on tech war or Taiwan; global bond markets are in their worst selloff in years with US 10Y at 4.59%, UK 30Y at 5.86%, and Japan 30Y at historic 4.085%; Fed is pivoting toward potential rate hikes as US CPI hits 3.8% and oil stays above $100 due to prolonged Iran war. Thailand outperformed with Q1 2026 GDP at 2.8% (vs 2.2% consensus), driven by private investment surge (+10.1%) and export frontloading, though momentum may moderate in Q2-Q3. InnovestX recommends selective buys in earnings-growth stocks (ADVANC, GULF, SCGP), defensive high-pricing-power plays (telecom, healthcare, commerce), and structural S-curve beneficiaries (clean energy, industrial estates).
