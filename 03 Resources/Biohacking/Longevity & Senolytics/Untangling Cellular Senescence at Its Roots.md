Cellular senescence — where damaged cells stop dividing but resist death and secrete inflammatory signals (SASP) — is being actively untangled at its mechanistic roots, with mitochondrial membrane lipid loss emerging as a key upstream driver of cellular aging. Oxidative stress regulators are implicated in accelerating cataract formation, while CRISPR-based RNA-targeting systems now offer on-demand cell killing, pointing toward precise senolytic therapies. Cyclarity's Phase 1 trial success and oxidized cholesterol excretion data signal near-clinical progress in clearing atherosclerotic plaques via 7-ketocholesterol removal. Parallel advances include mRNA therapies enhancing T-cell priming to destroy cancer, and a dedicated drug program targeting heart disease reversal — collectively representing a convergence of longevity interventions moving from bench to clinic.
