Mitochondrial aging is mechanistically linked to the degradation of key membrane lipids, suggesting lipid maintenance as a longevity target. Cellular senescence, oxidative stress, and cholesterol oxidation are emerging as interconnected hallmarks of aging being addressed through novel therapeutics. Cyclarity's Phase 1 trial success with oxidized cholesterol excretion represents a concrete clinical milestone in reversing cardiovascular aging. Cutting-edge approaches including CRISPR-based RNA targeting, mRNA cancer therapy via T cell priming, and standardized longevity physician education signal rapid medicalization of the longevity field.
