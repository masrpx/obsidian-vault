Mitochondrial aging is mechanistically linked to the depletion of key membrane lipids, suggesting lipid composition plays a central role in cellular energy decline with age. Omega-3 fatty acids show promise in alleviating kidney disease, likely through anti-inflammatory and membrane-stabilizing pathways. Cellular senescence research is advancing toward root-cause interventions, while CRISPR-based RNA-targeting systems and mRNA cancer therapies represent cutting-edge tools in precision medicine. Cyclarity's Phase 1 trial success (targeting 7-ketocholesterol removal) and efforts to develop drugs reversing heart disease signal accelerating progress in longevity biotech.
