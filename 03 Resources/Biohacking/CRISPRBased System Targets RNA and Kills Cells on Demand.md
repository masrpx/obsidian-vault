A CRISPR-based system has been developed that can target specific RNA sequences and trigger programmed cell death on demand, representing a major leap in precision medicine. This approach goes beyond traditional CRISPR DNA editing by operating at the RNA level, allowing conditional and reversible cellular control. The broader research landscape highlighted alongside this includes mitochondrial membrane lipid loss as a driver of aging, cellular senescence mechanisms, and novel mRNA cancer therapies that enhance T cell immune responses — all converging on the theme of programmable biological intervention for longevity and disease reversal.
