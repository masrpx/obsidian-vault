A drug candidate is being developed with the goal of reversing heart disease, representing a shift from managing symptoms to actively undoing cardiovascular damage. This aligns with broader longevity research trends including mitochondrial aging linked to membrane lipid loss, cellular senescence mechanisms, and oxidative stress pathways contributing to conditions like cataracts. Additional breakthroughs include a CRISPR-based RNA-targeting cell-kill system and an mRNA therapy that enhances T cell priming to destroy cancer. Cyclarity's drug targeting 7-ketocholesterol (a toxic oxidized lipid in arterial plaques) recently completed a successful Phase 1 trial, marking a concrete clinical milestone in reversing atherosclerosis.
