Cyclarity Therapeutics has completed a successful Phase 1 trial for a drug targeting oxidized cholesterol (7-ketocholesterol) to reverse heart disease, with new data showing measurable excretion of this toxic lipid from the body. The broader longevity research landscape is advancing rapidly across multiple fronts: CRISPR-based RNA-targeting systems capable of killing specific cells on demand, mRNA therapies enhancing T cell priming to destroy cancer, and new understanding of cellular senescence mechanisms at the root level. Mitochondrial aging is being linked to loss of key membrane lipids, suggesting lipid integrity is central to both cardiac and cellular longevity. China is also formalizing standardized physician education in longevity medicine, signaling mainstream clinical adoption is accelerating.
