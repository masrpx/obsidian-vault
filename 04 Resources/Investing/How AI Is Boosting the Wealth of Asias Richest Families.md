AI is increasingly being leveraged by Asia's wealthiest families to grow and manage their wealth, with key developments including DeepSeek expanding China's influence in open-source AI and escalating cyber threats posing risks to financial stability. Thailand is also facing economic headwinds, slashing its GDP forecast as a 3.78 trillion baht budget pushes national debt toward its ceiling. These converging forces — AI-driven wealth creation, geopolitical tech competition, and regional economic stress — are reshaping the investment landscape across Asia.
