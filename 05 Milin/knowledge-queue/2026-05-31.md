## 1. Growth hormone for musculoskeletal system repair
- source: https://peterattiamd.com/growth-hormone-for-musculoskeletal-system-repair/
- type: rss
- vault_path: 03 Resources/Biohacking
- relevance: แม็กสนใจ biohacking และ longevity — note นี้ช่วย calibrate ความเข้าใจเรื่อง hGH/secretagogues ที่มักถูก overhype ในวงการ biohacking โดยตรง เป็น critical thinking framework ก่อนพิจารณาใช้หรือลงทุนใน protocol เหล่านี้
- approved: false

Growth hormone (hGH) and GH secretagogues (tesamorelin, ibutamoren, ipamorelin) are widely marketed for tissue repair, anti-aging, and performance enhancement, but the clinical evidence in non-deficient adults is strikingly thin. The mechanistic rationale is real — GH raises IGF-1, activates mTOR/AKT pathways, and drives collagen synthesis in preclinical models — but systemic hGH is a blunt, body-wide intervention that doesn't deliver a precise local repair signal to injured tissue. The article separates three distinct questions: tissue repair acceleration (weak evidence), anti-catabolic protection during immobilization (plausible but narrow), and anti-aging replacement in non-deficient adults (biological extrapolation without convincing human outcomes). Legitimate medical use exists for documented GH-deficient patients and severe catabolic conditions (burns, HIV wasting), but this is categorically different from using hGH after routine orthopedic injury or as a longevity intervention. Existing human trials rarely show improvements in outcomes patients actually care about: pain, function, strength, or return to activity.
